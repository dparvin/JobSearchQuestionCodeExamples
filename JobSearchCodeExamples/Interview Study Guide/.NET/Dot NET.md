# .NET Interview Study Guide

This section contains information about .NET, including architecture, 
patterns, and best practices. It is intended to help prepare for .NET 
interviews and improve your understanding of designing scalable and 
maintainable systems using the .NET framework. It also includes examples 
of .NET designs I have done in the past, along with lessons learned and 
interview questions that might be asked about them.
