# Logic Puzzles Interview Study Guide

Below is a list of logic puzzles that can be used in interviews to assess 
a candidate's problem-solving and critical thinking skills. Each puzzle 
includes a description, a solution, and lessons learned from the experience 
as well as the answer I gave during an interview if it was different from 
the classic solution.
