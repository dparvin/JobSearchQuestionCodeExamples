# Architectural Patterns Guide

## Overview

Architectural patterns describe the high-level organization and structure
of an application. While design patterns typically solve problems within
a codebase, architectural patterns define how major components,
services, and layers interact with each other.

Architectural decisions affect scalability, maintainability, testability,
deployment, and how teams work with the system.
